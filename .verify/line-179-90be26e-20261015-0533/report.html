Not verified and risks

  • The backoff sleep is a plain tokio::time::sleep: a shutdown during a backoff is only noticed when the sleep ends, so a stop can wait up to 240 s on the 5th backoff before the Launch returns (no further tab is opened, as claimed). Spec decision 4 permits this, but SIGTERM latency may be long.
  • Every terminal-backed Launch now spends the full 1 s exit probe before spawned (seen: ~1.5 s from second tab.create to spawned); full 50-min suite not run to confirm no other case's timing moved.

  • C4 and C8 are verified only by unit/engine tests with a fake herdr, not through the release server.
  • Ticket events.jsonl files were not found in kept worlds after teardown (only conv files were); evidence was captured in-test via a verifier copy of the case instead.
